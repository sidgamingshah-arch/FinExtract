# Part 2 — Sheet Specifications

Per-model requirements, as supplied. Seven source sheets; six models plus a governance sheet.

| Source sheet | Model / purpose | Inputs to capture | Build logic | Key outputs | Special rules / acceptance tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EMI-based-Fixed** | Fixed-rate monthly EMI with EIR fee amortisation, journals, prepayment and partial-drawdown examples. | Rate; fee; start/end; gross amount; EMI; frequency; loan ID; basis; optional prepayment; drawdown. | Monthly month-end schedule. Gross interest and principal from EMI. Net opening = gross − fee. Solve EIR to zero final net balance. Fee amortisation = EIR interest − contractual interest. Produce origination, monthly, reporting and prepayment journals. | EIR; gross/net schedules; period/unamortised/cumulative fee; entries; prepayment write-off; drawdown fee allocation. | ACT/365; 54 rows in illustration. Final balances and fee = zero. Prepayment writes off remaining fee. Allocate fee to partial drawdown proportionately. |
| **Bullet Repayment-Fixed** | Fixed-rate bullet principal with periodic interest and EIR fee amortisation. | Rate; fee; start/end; gross amount; frequency; rate type; basis. | Interest paid periodically; no principal before maturity; full principal at maturity. Net opening = principal − fee. Solve EIR; amortisation is EIR interest less contractual interest. | EIR; gross/net schedules; fee amortisation and unamortised fee. | ACT/360; 12 periods. Terminal cash flow includes interest plus full principal. Amortisation total = fee. |
| **EMI based floating rate loan** | Legacy detailed monthly floating EMI with quarterly resets, commission netting and reporting cut-offs. | Tenor; dates; reset/payment frequency; amount; benchmark/rates; fee; commission; EMI; reporting dates. | Net opening = amount − fee + commission. Monthly gross and EIR schedules; map each row to its reset rate. Total receipt follows EMI; amortisation = EIR interest − contractual interest. Prorate amortisation at reporting cut-offs; calculate ROI/EIR analytics. | Quarter rates; monthly schedules; fee roll-forward; cut-off balances; ROI/EIR income and spreads. | Source mixes ACT/360 and ACT/365 and fixed row blocks. Rebuild parameterically and explicitly define the basis for each calculation. |
| **EMI based- Floating rate loans** | Equal-annual-principal floating loan with benchmark reset and modification result. | Amount; original benchmark/spread; dates; annual principal; fee; reset date/rate; reporting date; basis. | Original rate = benchmark + spread. Net opening = gross − fee. Solve original EIR; EIR spread = EIR − original rate. At reset: new rate = new benchmark + original spread; new EIR = new rate + original EIR spread. Rebuild future cash flows and compare revised PV with carrying amount. | Original/revised EIR; EIR spread; schedules; PV; modification gain/loss. | ACT/365. Equal annual principal. Source sign is PV − carrying amount. Original EIR spread remains fixed. |
| **Bullet -Floating rate loans** | Bullet floating loan with benchmark reset, revised EIR and modification result. | Amount; benchmark/spread; dates; bullet terms; fee; reset rate/date; reporting date; basis. | Same reset method as floating EMI, but no principal before maturity and full principal at maturity. Revalue revised future receipts and compute modification result. | Original/revised EIR; spread; schedules; fee; PV and modification result. | ACT/365. Terminal receipt includes interest plus principal. Final balances and fee = zero. |
| **CC Loan - SLM** | Cash-credit exposure with straight-line fee amortisation. | Rate; fee; facility dates; limit; utilisation; repayment timing; basis. | Interest on utilised amount until repayment. Fee amortisation = original fee × period days ÷ total facility days; unamortised fee rolls down. | Interest schedule; day-weighted SLM fee and unamortised balance. | ACT/365; source has five-month utilisation and 366-day fee period. Total fee must amortise to zero unless acceleration policy applies. |
| **Assumptions** | Narrative scope and policy guardrails. | Loan/rate type; EIR method; day-count; scenario exclusions; framework; disclaimer. | Make assumptions visible and feed them to controls. Sheet-specific terms override generic assumptions. | Version-controlled assumption list. | Do not apply the generic bullet/fixed/ACT-360 set to sheets that explicitly use floating rates or another basis. |
